ETHSafari

ETHSafari is a nine-day Ethereum event that moves from Nairobi to the Kenyan coast. The latest edition brought together more than 3,000 attendees, 150 speakers and 80 companies. I designed the event website and built it in WordPress.

• Approach

I moved away from the dark, neon look common to crypto conferences. The site uses a light background, large type, route coordinates and the event’s own humour. It feels like ETHSafari rather than a reusable conference theme.

The journey from Nairobi to Kilifi shapes the page. Visitors see the dates and route first, then the programme, tickets and previous editions. The archive covers five years, which gives new attendees useful context before they book.

Speakers, schedules, side events and vendor applications are managed in WordPress. The organisers can update information quickly during the final weeks before the event.
