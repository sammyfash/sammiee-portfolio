ICP Hub Kenya

ICP Hub Kenya trains developers, funds startups and runs events for the Internet Computer community in East Africa. I was part of the hub’s team, and I designed its website and built it in Webflow so the team could publish events, programmes and news without waiting for a developer.

• Approach

The hub speaks to three very different people. A developer wants training and grants, a student wants events and a way in, and a university or sponsor wants proof that the hub delivers. The homepage gives each of them a route in its first screen, then shows the proof: developers trained, grants secured, partner universities and startups incubated.

The look takes the Internet Computer’s purple and gradient and makes it local, with the hub’s own astronaut mascot, photos of the Nairobi community and a Kenyan flag ticker running across the hero.

Everything the team publishes often, events, programmes, projects and news, lives in Webflow CMS collections. When I handed the site over, the team could add a new event or programme themselves.

• Outcome

The site carried the hub’s outreach to developers, universities, sponsors and ecosystem partners during my time with the team. The original domain has since lapsed, so the case study includes a recorded walkthrough of the finished build.
